    How it works it out

    Seed is sold by weight. A new lawn from bare soil takes about 35 g of seed per square metre; thickening a thin lawn is lower, about 25 g. Tick the box and it adds half as much again, the way the RHS suggests, so enough comes up.

    Turf is sold by the roll, and a roll covers about a square metre. Rolls get cut to fit the edges, so it adds a little for waste and rounds up to whole rolls.

    New ground usually wants a bed of topsoil first. Put in a depth and it works out the volume, with a tenth on top because loose soil beds down.

    Rates and roll sizes vary, so every figure is one you can edit. Check the number on your own seed bag or with the turf yard. RHS: lawns from seed.
